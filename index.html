Methodology + source discipline

This prototype uses illustrative scores to demonstrate the decision flow; it does not claim current FOX audience delivery or campaign performance. Production data should retain source, geography, population, sample/base, collection date, confidence and usage rights at the metric level.

The production spine should join FOX, Tubi and Roku first-party catalog, schedule, viewing and campaign data to licensed audience, social, OOH, TV/CTV, search, display, audio, theatrical and international sources. Every score remains decomposable so planners can see the factors and override the recommendation.

Decision rule: audience fit selects the recommended title or entity; brand suitability, editorial adjacency and rights clearance remain an independent gate. News, politics, breaking news and sports rights are never silently penalized inside the audience-fit score.
